Notes and Ideas

- Morning shift uses supplies from the previous day
- You are the day shift that stocks the warehouse for the next day
- Night shift are Jack Stone (guard) and Kate (intern cleaner and supply stocker)

- Jack Stone
    - A strong and tough old guy
    - Keeps the truck drivers in line
    - Watches over the warehouse interior and exterior
    - Fixes broken stuff around the warehouse
    - Likes a stiff drink

- Kate
    - Heart of gold young girl
    - Intern making some money while shes in college
    - Stocks the cafeteria for the player with items he bought
    - Keeps cool items cool and hot items hot
    - Cleans the players gear and tools
    - Loves sweets
